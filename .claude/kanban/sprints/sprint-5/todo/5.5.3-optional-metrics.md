## Tarea

Add the six opt-in metric families, off by default. Design §4 (`optional`).

1. **`metrics/optional.py`**: `bleu` (BLEU-4, brevity penalty, add-one
   smoothing) and `rouge_l` (LCS F-measure), pure Python over
   `normalise()`d word tokens; `noise_sensitivity`,
   `context_entity_recall`, `toxicity` and `bias` with the judge.
2. **`metrics/prompts.py`**: `entities_messages`/`parse_entities` and
   `flag_messages`/`parse_flag`.
3. Register them in `METRICS` with `default_enabled = False`, re-export,
   `.api` files.

**Acceptance criteria**
- BLEU and ROUGE-L match hand-computed values (identical texts give 1.0,
  disjoint texts give 0.0, a partial overlap case) and ignore case and
  accents.
- `noise_sensitivity` reuses the memoised claims and chunk relevance of
  `JudgeSession` (no extra claim or relevance call when 5.5.2 families run
  too).
- None of the six runs unless included; `include` turns each one on.
- `uv run just check` is green.
