## Tarea

Add the judge-based metric families and their prompts. Design §4
(`judge`, `prompts`, `judged`) and §7.

1. **`metrics/prompts.py`**: the `*_messages` builders and strict
   `parse_*` validators for claims, verify, relevance and questions, with
   the JSON shapes of design §4. Indexes are 1-based; a wrong type, a
   missing key, an out-of-range or repeated index, or a verdict count that
   differs from the input raises `MetricError`.
2. **`JudgeSession`**: `answer_claims`, `expected_claims`, `verify`
   (memo by key) and `chunk_relevance` (one call for all the retrieved
   chunks).
3. **`metrics/judged.py`**: `faithfulness`, `answer_relevance` (n=3
   generated questions, cosine with the question embedding through
   `MetricServices.embedder`, 0 when noncommittal), `context_precision`
   (average precision), `context_recall`, `answer_correctness`
   (precision, recall, F1) and `citations` (precision, recall), plus
   `citation_targets`, `average_precision` and `cosine`.
4. **Citation mapping**: verify against the public `GenerationResult` of
   `hermes_generator` (and the prompt the strategy builds) whether a `[n]`
   marker is the n-th context piece, 1-based. If the result carries explicit
   citations, use them through `MetricInput.citations` instead. Write what
   you found in the Manuel section.
5. Register the families in `METRICS`, re-export, `.api` files.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Tests use a scripted fake `JudgeClient` (replies by purpose) and
  `HashEmbedder`. Every formula is checked by hand on small cases,
  including no claims, no citations, all irrelevant chunks and
  noncommittal answers.
- Claims of the answer are requested once per question even with
  `faithfulness`, `answer_correctness` and `citations` all enabled.
- Malformed replies (not JSON, wrong shape, misaligned indexes) give
  `status="error"` with the raw reply in `calls`, and the other families
  still run.
- With `EchoJudgeClient` every judge family ends with a valid status
  (`error` is acceptable, a crash is not).
- `uv run just check` is green.

**Carry-over from 5.5.1** (Carlos's review):
- `JudgeSession.ask` catches only `ProviderError`, `ValueError` and `MetricError`. A `parse` that raises a bare `KeyError`/`TypeError`, or a client that returns a non-`str`, escapes `ask` unlogged and unmemoised (the raw reply is lost and the judge is called again). Close it: every `parse_*` raises only `MetricError`, and `ask` also turns any other exception into a logged, memoised `MetricError`. Carlos pins both cases.
